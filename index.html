How to Play

Navigate neon mazes to collect energy orbs, find keys, activate switches and reach the exit portal before time runs out.

  • Move: Arrow keys / WASD / On-screen D-Pad / Swipe
  • Abilities: Dash, Phase Shift (through special walls), Scanner (reveal secrets)
  • Avoid moving hazards and lasers. Use switches to open doors.

Tips: Explore for hidden rooms, use abilities sparingly, and try Time Trial or Endless for replay.
